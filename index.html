Before you upload
  1. Check that your video plays fully on your device.
  2. For the best phone and Smart TV compatibility, use MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio.
  3. If your video is MOV, WebM or another format, convert it to MP4 first. Choose MP4 as the output format and H.264/AAC if those options are available.

Free converters: CloudConvert · FreeConvert

Maximum file size: 500 MB. Check the converted video before uploading. Uploads remain private until approved.
