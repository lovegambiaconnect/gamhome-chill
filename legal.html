COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Community rules & terms

Respect creators and viewers

Use Gamhome&Chill lawfully and respectfully. Do not upload or share content you do not own or have permission to distribute.

Prohibited content

Do not upload illegal material, content involving minors in a sexual or exploitative context, non-consensual intimate imagery, harassment, threats, hate content, or material that violates another person's rights.

Creator uploads and moderation

Creator uploads remain private while under review. Gamhome&Chill may approve, reject, restrict or remove content that violates these rules or applicable law.

Monetization and payouts

Creator monetization requires 10,000 qualified streams and platform approval. The minimum payout is USD 20 equivalent, paid in Gambian dalasi (GMD) on the monthly cycle after approval. The exchange rate used is the payout provider's rate at processing time. Earnings are subject to applicable terms, verification, fraud checks and provider availability.

Accounts and security

Keep your account credentials secure. Do not impersonate another person, manipulate stream counts, interfere with platform systems or attempt unauthorized access.

Changes and contact

These rules may be updated as the platform develops. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised rules.
